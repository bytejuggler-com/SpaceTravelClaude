Space Travel · Interactive explainers

How the demo works

Space Travel is a real-time demo in the spirit of the Amiga and PC demoscene: fifteen procedural space scenes, about five minutes, synced to a tracker tune. These pages take each scene apart. Every page runs the demo's real shaders live in your browser, then explains the ideas behind them one at a time — from first principles, with small figures you can play with — and ends with step-by-step pseudo-code and the actual source.

Companion to the printed art book (SpaceTravel-ArtBook.pdf). Works offline: each page is a single file. Needs a browser with WebGL2 (any current Edge, Chrome or Firefox).

How to read a page

Live. The scene's real shaders, ported line for line to WebGL2. Scrub the time, change the parameters.
Background. Short chapters, one idea each, with an interactive figure and a "Try:" hint.
Step by step. The full algorithm as pseudo-code, each step linked to the idea that explains it.
Source. The actual GLSL and C++ from the repository, plus a glossary of every term used.
